# Rocket League fan-art pilot

Date: 2026-10-01. Status: recognizable-game direction approved by the owner;
prompts below are production drafts, not generated or integrated assets.

The owner wants art that visibly looks like Rocket League, including Octane,
Fennec and Batmobile. Use those names directly. Match the recognizable in-game car
forms and environments while authoring new compositions, rather than copying the
same stadium/ball shot four times. Verify car geometry against references during
generation review; a prompt alone cannot guarantee exact visual fidelity.

## Shot plan

| Role | Subject / camera | Scene | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero, dark | Octane, low sideline camera | Champions Field at blue hour, aerial challenge | Recognizable play and cinematic energy |
| Minimal, dark | Fennec, elevated rear three-quarter view | Quiet freeplay arena, small car in a broad field | Clean daily desktop, at least 70% negative space |
| Mood, dark | 2016 Batmobile, low rear-quarter camera | Rainy Neo Tokyo arena, restrained reflections | Strong different car silhouette and atmosphere |
| Alt, light | Octane and Fennec, high oblique aerial view | Sunny beach arena, patterned field and ocean | Brighter palette and a genuinely different viewpoint |

Use coherent materials and restrained highlights across the set. Leave the left
15% and bottom 8% calm. Keep subjects safe for focal crops; review 16:9, 16:10,
3:2, 21:9, 32:9 and any authored portrait. No HUD, scoreboards with readable text,
title logos, watermarks or team/sponsor decals. Car shapes remain recognizable.

## Full landscape draft prompts

### Hero: Octane aerial at blue hour

```text
Create a premium 16:9 desktop wallpaper as original Rocket League fan art. Show
an unmistakable Rocket League Octane in a new cinematic moment at Champions
Field during blue hour, captured from a low sideline camera just above the turf.
The compact blue Octane is lifting into an aerial challenge at the center-right,
with its correct recognizable body proportions, exposed wheels and roof shape;
the Rocket League ball is slightly above and ahead of it, separated clearly from
the roof. Use a short restrained boost trail, warm stadium floodlights against
cool evening air, and believable stylized in-game materials. The camera should
feel like a deliberately composed spectator photograph, with a broad readable
pitch and distant curved arena enclosure, not a close-up of a generic sports car.
Keep all important car and ball detail within the central 80% of the width and
middle 70% of the height. Preserve a low-detail left 15% for desktop icons and a
quiet bottom 8% for the taskbar. Smooth banding-free sky, no crushed shadows,
crisp car geometry, calm distant crowd lighting, no excessive particles. No HUD,
readable text, title treatment, logos, sponsor decals, watermarks, lens dirt,
chromatic aberration, heavy vignette, borders or duplicated wheels. This is newly
composed fan art with the recognizable Rocket League visual language, not a
recreation of existing official key art.
```

### Minimal: Fennec in quiet freeplay

```text
Create a refined dark 16:9 desktop wallpaper as original Rocket League fan art.
Show one unmistakable Rocket League Fennec in a quiet freeplay arena, seen from
an elevated rear three-quarter camera looking gently across the pitch. Preserve
the Fennec's correct boxy compact body, flat roof, squared rear and recognizable
in-game wheels; use a clean unbranded pearl finish with a subtle cool edge light.
Place the car small near 62% across and 56% down, surrounded by at least 70%
negative space. The arena surface has soft charcoal-blue color, extremely faint
field markings and a restrained pool of light beneath the car. Distant walls
fade into a smooth navy atmosphere; no large ball, busy crowd or dramatic boost
trail. The result should feel like a quiet Rocket League training session and a
beautiful clean desktop rather than an advertisement. Keep the left 15% and
bottom 8% especially calm. Smooth banding-free tonal transitions, readable dark
form, precise car geometry, no noise, no HUD, readable text, logos, sponsor decals,
watermarks, heavy vignette, borders, duplicated objects or distorted wheels.
```

### Mood: Batmobile in rainy Neo Tokyo

```text
Create a cinematic dark 16:9 desktop wallpaper as original Rocket League fan art,
featuring the recognizable 2016 Batmobile car as it appears in Rocket League,
with its long low body, broad armored profile and correct wheel layout. Show it
from a low rear-quarter camera, positioned center-right on the Rocket League
Neo Tokyo arena pitch after a rain shower. Preserve the characteristic car form
instead of substituting a generic supercar. Compose a new quiet between-matches
moment: restrained cyan and warm magenta light from the arena structure reflected
on the damp surface, delicate distant mist, and a few clean architectural shapes.
The car is the clear silhouette; the background is atmospheric and softly resolved.
Any distant sign panels must be abstract light panels with no lettering. No
characters, logos, HUD, readable text, sponsor decals, watermark or copied film
poster composition. Keep the left 15% low-detail and the bottom 8% calm. Protect
the car inside the central crop-safe area with room around its entire silhouette.
Smooth banding-free shadows that retain detail, realistic reflections restrained
enough for desktop use, no heavy vignette, lens dirt, chromatic aberration,
duplicated wheels, melted body panels or excessive particle effects.
```

### Light alternate: beach-arena aerial

```text
Create a bright elegant 16:9 desktop wallpaper as original Rocket League fan art.
Use a high oblique aerial spectator viewpoint over a sunny Rocket League beach
arena, with pale turquoise ocean beyond the enclosure, warm sand tones and a
clean geometric pitch. An unmistakable Rocket League Octane and Fennec are small
on the center-right of the field, one approaching the Rocket League ball while
the other turns gently into position. Preserve the correct distinct body shapes
and wheel proportions, with simple blue and warm orange unbranded finishes. The
cars and ball should be clearly readable but occupy little of the frame. Let
the broad field pattern, ocean horizon and soft afternoon shadows create an
entirely different composition from a ground-level stadium shot. Keep the left
15% and bottom 8% quiet, and all important subjects within the central crop-safe
area. Premium stylized in-game rendering, soft clean sunlight, smooth banding-free
sky and water, no crowded beach details, no HUD, readable text, title logo,
sponsor decals, watermark, borders, heavy vignette, excessive boost trails,
duplicated cars or deformed wheels. Newly composed fan art, not official key art.
```

## Companion edit prompts

For each approved landscape, use it as the reference. The landscape must be visually
reviewed first; do not generate companion crops from a failed car design.

Ultrawide extension:

```text
Extend the attached Rocket League fan-art landscape into a 3:1 panoramic master.
Preserve the existing cars, ball, body geometry, scale, viewpoint, lighting and
colors exactly. Add coherent arena, field, sky or ocean at the sides without
duplicating cars, balls, landmarks or light fixtures. Keep the subject safely
inside the central crop area for both 21:9 and 32:9 renditions. Maintain quiet
left and bottom desktop zones, smooth banding-free gradients and consistent
perspective. No seam, repeated scenery, HUD, text, logos or watermark. Do not
stretch the original image or crop out the car silhouette.
```

Optional portrait recomposition (author separately only for selected shots):

```text
Use the attached Rocket League fan-art landscape as the visual reference to
create a new 9:16 portrait composition. Preserve the featured car's recognizable
body, wheels, materials and the established arena palette and lighting. Recompose
the camera and environment so the entire main car silhouette remains comfortably
inside the frame, with clear space above and a quiet bottom taskbar zone. Do not
merely crop or stretch the landscape. Preserve believable field and arena
perspective, smooth gradients and readable shadows. No extra cars or wheels,
duplicated ball, HUD, text, logos, sponsor decals, watermark or visible seams.
```

## Review and distribution

Reject generic substitute cars, incorrect bodies/wheels, unreadable silhouettes,
repeated viewpoints, excessive bloom, noisy gradients or unsafe crops. Show the
four pilot renders together at thumbnail and full size before expanding the catalog.

The owner has approved named fan-art creation. Public distribution remains a separate
release check: [Epic's Fan Content Policy](https://legal.epicgames.com/epicgames/fan-art-policy)
covers qualifying non-commercial fan content, requires a disclaimer and prohibits
implied endorsement. Check applicability and any separate Batmobile rights before
including the pack in a public or monetized release. Calling something promotional
does not itself establish permission. Do not extract game assets or present these
images as official publisher artwork.
